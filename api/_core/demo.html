Do you write like Austen, Emerson or Henry Ford?

Paste something you wrote. It's measured on nine features of style (sentence length and rhythm, pronouns, questions, paragraph shape) and compared with 37 public-domain writers: novelists, essayists, memoirists, and business and advice writers, from Austen to Franklin to Emily Post. Counted, not guessed by a model.

Use text you wrote without AI help, or you'll measure the AI. Nothing is stored or logged: the text is measured in memory and discarded.

Open source (MIT): voice-profile-mcp on GitHub. Want a full voice profile and draft checks in ChatGPT or Claude? See the Voice Profile plugin. This compares measurable style only. It says nothing about quality, personality, or the authors as people.
