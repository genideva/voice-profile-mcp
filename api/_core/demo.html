The Style Notebook

Do you write like Austen, Emerson or Henry Ford?

Write or paste something of yours on the page. It's measured on nine features of style (sentence length and rhythm, pronouns, questions, paragraph shape) and compared with 37 public-domain writers: novelists, essayists, memoirists, and business and advice writers. Counted, not guessed by a model.

Name: youAssignment: your own words

Use writing you did without AI help, or you'll measure the AI. Nothing is stored or logged: your text is measured in memory and discarded.

Open source (MIT): voice-profile-mcp on GitHub. Want a full voice profile and draft checks in ChatGPT or Claude? See the Voice Profile plugin.
The grade marks resemblance, not quality. It says nothing about personality, or the writers as people.
